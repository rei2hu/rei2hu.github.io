'just ask ai'

A while ago, I needed to have a service performed by a law enforcement agency. Similar to something like medallion signature guarantees, this is something that can only be performed by specific people/institutions, but I'll leave out what the specific service is for reasons.

Now, this was the first time I had to do this, so I went online and did a bunch of searching to see how I could request it properly. After hours of research, I was unable to find a primary source that mentioned the service - neither police station, sheriff's department, nor other websites had any information. What I did come across was a several year old post that mentioned that the police station provided this, so I decided to give them a call.

I don't know how common this is, but before I called them this time, I had never called the police ever. What I understood was that there are emergency numbers like 112 or 911 or 999, and non-emergency numbers which are… I didn't know; time for another search. Another search later and I didn't find anything explicitly labeled as the non-emergency number, but I found an office number and ended up calling that.

After some time on-hold/in queue, I got to someone and mentioned that I was looking for this service and whether or not is was provided. The person on the phone said they remember another department providing that service, so I asked for that departments number, received it, thanked the person, and called the new number. In hindsight, I wonder if I could've asked for a transfer - but I don't really know how phone systems work.

I called the new number and someone else picked up. I gave the same introduction and ask, and was told that they didn't offer that service anymore, and said that I could look online to figure out who offered it. I responded that I did spend time looking it up, and they talked over me and said I didn't look hard enough and that I could "ask ai". They then mentioned the sheriff's office might offer it, so I asked if they had the number and got the same response about asking ai. Great.

I then went to look up the number of the sheriff's office, and gave them a call. Another person picked up and I asked them the same thing and they said they offered it. I scheduled an appointment a couple days later and got it done.

Remember that I looked at both the police station and sheriff departments' websites during my initial search and nothing came up. Furthermore, I went ahead and asked AI (chatgpt in this case) whether the service was offered by these places, and could only get generic answers - stuff like "yes, this service is typically offered by all law enforcement agencies to civilians for a small fee but I could not find any information about if this specific institution offers it or not". Very cool.
