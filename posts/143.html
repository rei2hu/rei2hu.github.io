framework laptop upgrade evaluation

I was recently looking to upgrade my framework laptop because I felt like the cpu was not good enough for my current uses. I currently have the Intel Core i5-1240p.

Taking a look at the marketplace, I see that they no longer offer mainboards that rely on DDR4 ram, which I'm currently using, so I'll have to purchase ram alongside the new mainboard. In fact, they have mainboards that support DDR5 ram in stock and upcoming mainboards that will support LPCAMM2 LPDDR5X which is much different.

In terms of buying ram, I'm leaning towards the LPCAMM2 solutions just because it's newer - the downside being that it only allows one slot so I'll end up replacing the ram fully if I decide to upgrade there as opposed to adding another stick on mainboards that support DDR5 (ignoring the possibility of already leveraging dual channel).

Now to evaluate the actual upgrade price compared with purchasing a new machine with similar power, though current hardware prices are outrageous.

I'm cheap, so I'm looking at the Intel Core Ultra 5 325 which is $449. Meanwhile, a 32GB LPCAMM2 from Framework is apparently $800, though they're sold out, and apparently prices go up to \$2000 on newegg??? Crazy… I'll just use \$800, thank you, which means a total cost of \$1250.

Searching around results in a few laptops with those specifications, though some use Intel Core Ultra 7 355 which is better.

  1. HP EliteBook 8 G2i 14 inch Notebook Next Gen AI PC - Customizable
    • \$3400 with cheapest customizations, assuming 32GB onboard ram is LPDDR5X (the supposed DDR5 option is more expensive anyways)
  2. Lenovo Slim 7i Aura Edition
    • $2050 with Intel Core Ultra 7 355, but discounted to $1550
  3. Galaxy Book6 Enterprise Edition
    • \$1880 with Intel Core Ultra 7 355
  4. ASUS Vivobook S14 Laptop (2026)
    • \$1600 with Intel Core Ultra 7 355, and 16GB DDR5 16GB LPDDR5X
  5. HP OmniBook 5 Laptop Next Gen AI 16T-CD000, 16"
    • \$1050 DDR5 only

Comparing against the upgrade price of \$1250, I would say there appears to be a decent amount of savings though all of the new laptops come with other stuff that may or may not be valuable.

And of course, how could I forget looking at a new framework laptop, which is apparently \$2000 when only including the new cpu and ram. So still cheaper, though.

In the end, I've decided to avoid upgrading for now just because the prices are high. Furthermore, since I initially posted wrote this, there has been a refurbished Framework 13 option, which costs around \$650 for a comparable Intel Core Ultra 5 125H and 32GB DDR5 ram (\$300 and \$350 respectively). I still won't buy, since everything requires new ram sticks and I cannot stomach the 4x+ increase. Here's to hoping the AI bubble pops and there's a fire sale soon…
